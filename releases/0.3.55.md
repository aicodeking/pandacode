# A tidier sidebar

## New

- **Sections.** Active lists tasks that are running, waiting on you, failed or rate-limited. Needs review comes next, then Pinned, Chats and your project folders. Completed sits at the bottom, closed by default, showing 10 tasks and then 25 more at a time. A task you open stays where it is instead of jumping to another section.
- **Sort and view menu.** Sort tasks by hand, by recent activity or by when they were created. Sort projects by recent activity, the order you added them, name, or by hand (drag a folder, or use Alt+↑/↓). Choose how many tasks each folder shows (3, 6, 10 or 15), and group by project or show one flat list. Your choice is remembered.
- **Filter field.** Press `/` to search task titles, branches, pull requests and project names. Matches inside collapsed folders and the closed Completed section show too.
- **Task and folder menus.** Right-click a task or click its … button to pin, move, rename, mark it read or unread, start a new task from its branch, copy its branch, path or ID, or complete, archive or delete it. Folders get New task, Collapse other projects, Reveal in Finder and Remove project. Complete, archive and unpin can be undone from the toast or with ⌘Z.
- **Keyboard.** The task list is one Tab stop: arrow keys move through it, ⌘1…9 open a task, ⌘⇧[ and ⌘⇧] step between tasks, and Shift+F10 opens the menu.

## Changed

- **More compact.** Two-line rows went from 71px to about 50px and one-line rows from 32px to 28px. Tasks that need you show a word: Approval, Answer, Failed, or Limited with its reset time. Unread tasks show a dot and a bold title.
- **Sleeker menus.** Menus use the app's glass material, and the current choice gets a check mark.
- **Easier to read.** Grey sidebar text over light wallpapers is darker.

## Fixed

- New tasks no longer sink to the bottom after you reorder tasks by hand.
- Project folders no longer jump when one of their tasks starts running.
- Clicking a task no longer reopens a folder you had collapsed.
- Undo after completing a task puts it back exactly where it was.
- Sorting projects by "Added" really uses the order you added them.

## Known limitations

- In a very narrow sidebar, the flat list shows each task's project as an icon only; its name is in the tooltip.
