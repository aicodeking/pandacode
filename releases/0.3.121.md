# Safer lasting permissions, a clearer Workers panel

## Changed

- **ZCode asks before a lasting permission.** In Full mode, ZCode no longer approves a request by itself when the only choice offered is a lasting, project-wide rule. It asks you instead, and team workers decline it. Unattended Full ZCode runs, such as schedules, now wait for a person when this happens.
- **Phones can't grant lasting permissions without full access.** Without **Full access for remote tasks**, a phone or other remote device can no longer approve a request whose only choice is a lasting permission, on any thread. It can still decline it. Bots are deliberately unchanged: a bot that asks you in chat can still approve a lasting permission when you allow it there.
- **Side threads re-checked from a phone.** Asking a By the way (`/btw`) question from a phone in a side thread that has since come to lead a team needs **Full access for remote tasks**, as other team actions do.

## Fixed

- **Schedules keep their remote mark.** A schedule saved or edited from a phone, or created by an agent, is saved together with its "saved remotely" mark, so one can no longer be stored without the other.
- **Bot team turns are checked in one step.** The check that a bot may run a team turn now happens in a single step, so a thread can't become a team lead between the check and the turn. If the thread's saved team can't be read, the turn is refused and the bot request it carried stays queued.
- **Workers stay in a live turn.** The Workers list no longer disappears from a live turn after the next tool call.

## Improved

- **Workers panel.**
  - A failed worker's error is shown in red, wraps instead of being cut off, and shows the full text in a tooltip.
  - The danger colour adapts to your wallpaper.
  - An info button explains the deploy and scheduling exception (tools that always ask are declined for workers). Click or tap it to show the explanation.
  - Escape hides an opened info tip first, without closing the panel.
  - An opened tip closes when its panel closes or the note it belongs to goes away.
  - Dismissing the access note moves focus to the worker list.
  - The key hint lists every key and wraps to a second line in a narrow panel instead of being cut off.
  - A dot now separates the counts in the worker tally, as in "1 needs you · 2 working".
  - Rows grow with larger text sizes.
- **Sticky transcript header.** Controls you move to with the keyboard are no longer hidden under the sticky transcript header.

## Known limitations

- On the phone, the refusal for a lasting-only permission appears only after **Approve** is tapped; the button is not disabled beforehand.
