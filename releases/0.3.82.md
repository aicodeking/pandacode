# Multi-machine orchestration

## Added

- **Agents can run work on your other computers.** Turn on Settings → Remote access → Agent orchestration (off by default). An agent can then ask to link one of your computers signed in to PandaCode Connect, and launch, message, stop and wait on threads there.
  - **You decide every link.** A card above the composer opens a two-step Link a machine dialog: pick the computer, then its access (Read only, Ask first or Full access). Agents never link on their own, and you can change or unlink a computer in Settings at any time.
  - **A thread never gets more access than the agent that launched it.** It runs in Full access only when both the link and the launching task are Full access; a plan-mode task cannot launch at all. Agents can only touch threads they launched.
  - **Threads show up like any other task.** Each one is listed in the sidebar with its own transcript and approvals, in a lineage strip above the composer (with Stop and “Waiting on N threads”), and in a “N threads · M working” card in the transcript.
  - **The agent hears back automatically.** When its threads finish, the launching task wakes once with their results, even across a quit or restart, and never twice.
  - Everything travels over the existing end-to-end encrypted Connect relay; nothing new goes over the network.

## Known limitations

- Launching and messaging threads don’t ask for approval when the task runs in Full access, like every other PandaCode tool. The link’s access level still limits what the thread can do.
- Threads on a computer that never comes back online keep showing as running.
- Orchestration has been tested against simulated hosts; try it on your own pair of computers before relying on it for long jobs.
