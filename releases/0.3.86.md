# Inset panels and calmer motion

## New

- **The conversation sits in its own panel.** The sidebar now shares one frame colour with the window, and the conversation is an inset panel with soft 12px corners and an 8px margin, so the sidebar flows around it. The inspector is a second panel beside it with a small gap, and Settings uses the same frame and panel.
- **Calmer thread switching.** Opening a thread fades its content in once, over about 200 ms with a slight rise. Clicking quickly through threads never dims the transcript again, and on a first visit the previous thread stays on screen, dimmed, for up to 150 ms instead of flashing blank.

## Changed

- The inspector slides in gently when it opens. When it closes it fades out first and the conversation widens afterwards, so text no longer rewraps underneath it. Keyboard focus returns to its toggle.
- Sidebar rows ease their hover and selection colours in and out. Opening Settings, collapsing the sidebar and opening the GitHub view fade softly instead of cutting, and switching to Board changes the layout in one step.
- With the sidebar collapsed, and in the Board and GitHub views, the conversation runs edge to edge.
- The top row lines up: the conversation header and the inspector's tabs centre on the window controls and the sidebar's top row.
- Find in thread (⌘F) sits in the empty header row.
- Inspector diffs keep a small gap at the panel's edge.

## Known limitations

- On macOS the PandaCode name is hidden in sidebars narrower than 280px (including the default width) instead of being cut off.
- The light frame under the macOS window material and the Reduce Transparency fallback have not been checked on a real machine yet.
