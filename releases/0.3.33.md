# Menu bar

## New

- **PandaCode in the menu bar.** A panda icon shows how many agents are running. Its menu lists running tasks, tasks that need your approval or answer, and tasks that finished since you last looked; click one to open it.
- **Plan usage at a glance.** The same menu shows each signed-in agent's plan limits, such as Claude's 5-hour and weekly windows or Codex's limits. They are checked only when you open the menu or pick **Refresh usage**, never in the background.

## Known limitations

- The "finished, not viewed" list is kept in memory and starts empty after PandaCode restarts.
- Each section shows up to 10 tasks.
