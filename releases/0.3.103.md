# Council: scored review and fix loops

## Added

- Council brings independent reviewers to an agent team, scores each review out of 10, and asks the lead to fix findings and convene fresh rounds until the target or round limit is reached.
- A compact composer strip shows the current round, lowest score, target, and expandable reviewer results, including first and final scores. Open a reviewer directly from its row.
- Council settings control the target, round limit, reviewer count, and whether new threads start with Council on. When the team needs a decision, choose Keep fixing, Lower target, or Accept and finish; raise the round limit when more preset rounds are available.

## Fixed

- Changes after a review make its scores stale, so earlier passing scores cannot finish newly changed work without review.
- Canceling Council setup clears its pending state so setup can be retried. Actions with no lower target or higher round preset are hidden, avoiding empty pickers.

## Known limitations

- Live review and fix loops using paid models have not been validated for this release.
- Full cross-platform installer validation on real hardware has not been completed.
- Council cannot detect edits made outside PandaCode, or shell/script edits that are not reported through file tracking.
