# Sidebar: Active, Finished and Settled

## Fixed

- **No more missing tasks.** The sidebar now has only three states: **Active**, **Finished** and **Settled** (plus Pinned). Tasks that were not running and not settled could fall out of Finished into separate Chats and Tasks lists. Every one of them (new, stopped, interrupted, staged on the board, or a chat) now lists under Finished until you settle it.
- Running tasks stay in Active with the blinking status, as before.
- Finished follows **Sort and view**. Under Manual, tasks you have never dragged come first, newest first, and you can drag rows or use Move up/down. It shows the chosen number of tasks per section, with **Show more** for the rest.
- Pinning the open task moves it to Pinned straight away.

## Known limitations

- The separate Chats and Tasks lists are gone. Chats now list in Finished, and each row names its project.
