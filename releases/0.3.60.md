# Choose how team workers are picked, right from the prompt box

## New

- **Worker-models picker in the composer.** When a task has a team and model routing is on, a chip next to **Team** shows how Panda Router picks each worker's model: **Cheapest that passes**, **Balanced** or **Best quality**. Click it to switch. Your choice is saved and stays on whatever you used last, across tasks and restarts, and it matches Settings → Agent teams → Model routing in both directions.

## Improved

- The chip keeps its short label in narrow windows, follows the wallpaper styling of the other toolbar pickers, and never takes focus away from what you are typing.
- Routing status stays consistent when you change the mode quickly or while the profiler downloads: an older status reply can no longer undo a newer choice, and a status that fails to load retries on its own.

## Known limitations

- The picker is hidden for tasks running on another computer; their team is set up on that computer.
