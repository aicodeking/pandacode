# Shorter model lists for teams

## New

- **Long model catalogs stay short for teams.** A harness with more than 10 models (OpenCode without a saved selection, Devin, Kilo and others) now gives teams only its 10 most recently used models, plus any you add. Panda Router never routes to the rest. OpenCode with a model selection saved in Settings keeps that selection.
- **Add model…** Settings → Agent teams → Model routing shows "Showing N of M models · Add model…" on long harnesses in **Models for teams**, and **Rank models** has an **Add model…** button. The dialog searches the harness's full list; turn a model on to let teams use it. Recently used models are always included.

## Known limitations

- A harness with more than 10 models that you have never used gives teams no models until you use some or add them.
- Added models are saved per window profile (local storage), not synced between computers.
