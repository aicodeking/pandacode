# Agents in the dock

## New

- **Agents in the action dock.** Architect & workers and subagents now open from an icon in the dock on the right instead of floating pills at the top of the thread. A badge shows how many agents are running. Hover the icon to open the panel; it stays open while your pointer is on the icon or the panel. Click to keep it open, Esc to close.

## Improved

- **Conversation starts at the top.** The thread no longer reserves space at the top for the old floating bars, and the soft fade at the top edge is gone. Text now scrolls straight up to the window edge.
