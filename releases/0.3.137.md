# Panda Router prefers newer models

## Fixed

Panda Router now recommends newer models over their predecessors when both are available in the routing pool and the newer model costs the same or less.

- **GPT-6.2 Sol** supersedes GPT-6.1 Sol and GPT-6 Sol.
- **GPT-6.2 Astra** supersedes GPT-6 Astra.
- **Claude Fable 5.2** supersedes Claude Fable 5.1 and Claude Fable 5.

Older models remain available for routing when their replacement is missing from the installed harnesses, excluded, or paused.
