# Panda bot icons and simpler bot settings

## New

- **Panda icons for bots.** Pick from 10 panda icons in different colors, or upload your own image (PNG, JPEG, WebP or GIF up to 5 MB, cropped to a square). The icon shows in the sidebar, the conversation header, the welcome screen and the web chat.
- **Bot settings look like Settings.** The bot settings window now has the same layout as PandaCode's Settings: sections down the left (Profile, Channels, Schedules, Memory, Activity) and grouped rows on the right. Conversations, rules and the activity log now live together under Activity.

## Known limitations

- The web chat page shows the bot's first letter instead of a panda icon. Uploaded images do show there.
- Bots that use an emoji icon keep it. Pick a panda or upload an image in the bot's Profile to change it.
