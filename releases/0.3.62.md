# A floating action dock

## New

- **Action dock.** Run, Deploy, Moodboards, Browser, Terminal, the inspector toggle and Task actions are no longer in the header. They float in a frosted dock at the right edge of the conversation, stacked like an app's tab bar, so the top bar holds just the project and its breadcrumb.
  - **Position:** the dock sits centred between the header and the prompt box, beside the inspector when it is open. In narrow windows and on the Board and GitHub views it becomes a row in the header.
  - **Active panels:** open panels (inspector, terminal, browser) show an accent fill. A soft highlight glides to whatever you hover or reach with the keyboard.
  - **Labels:** they appear after a short pause, then follow you instantly from icon to icon.
  - **Motion:** items spring in and out as they appear (Run, the update prompt), and the dock arrives with a quick staggered entrance once per session. All of it is instant with Reduce Motion on.
  - **Keyboard:** the dock is a single Tab stop. Use the arrow keys, Home and End to move through it, and Escape closes a label without affecting anything else.

## Improved

- The conversation and prompt box leave room for the dock, so nothing you can click ever sits under it.
- The Architect & workers bar sits beside the dock.
- In a shared bot channel, the Channel settings button keeps its own row.
