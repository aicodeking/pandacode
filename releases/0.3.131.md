# Gemini 4 Neon model card and harness routing

## Added

- Embedded Panda Router model card and harness launch-ID mappings for **Gemini 4 Neon**, allowing automatic team routing and architect worker dispatch to rate and select it when advertised by an installed harness and enabled for the team:
  - Antigravity: `gemini-4-neon` and its effort variants (`gemini-4-neon-low`, `gemini-4-neon-medium`, `gemini-4-neon-high`, `gemini-4-neon-xhigh`)
  - OpenCode: `openrouter/google/gemini-4-neon`

## Known limitations

- Capability scores and latency are initial conservative routing estimates (`se: 1.3`, calibrated between Gemini 4 Hydrogen and Gemini 4 Carbon), paired with verified API list pricing and limits.
- Model cards do not guarantee account access; Gemini 4 Neon availability depends on your Antigravity plan and account entitlement, or your OpenRouter/provider API access when using OpenCode, and it becomes eligible for routing only when advertised by the installed harness and enabled for the team.
