# PandaCode Connect is fast on slow networks

## Faster

- **Remote threads open in about a second, even on 3G.** Messages between your computers and phone are now compressed before they are encrypted, and a remote thread shows its newest messages first while older ones fill in above without moving your place. On a simulated 3G link (400 kbit/s, 400 ms round trip), connecting and opening a 5.5 MB thread went from 164 s to 2.0 s on the desktop and from 5.1 s to 2.0 s on the phone; opening a thread on an existing connection takes 0.7–0.9 s.
- **Reconnecting is quicker.** Missed messages are requested together with the reconnect, the phone retries straight away after a drop, and the desktop no longer pauses after reconnecting. On the same 3G link, reconnect went from 3.8 s to 2.3 s.
- **The host stays responsive.** Serving a long history to another device no longer holds up the host's own app, and several devices watching one task stay within the relay's limits instead of losing live updates.

## Fixed

- Long remote threads no longer keep a gap in their older history, including after a restart.
- A very long newest message that was shortened to fit the first page is fetched in full afterwards, also after a restart.
- The phone gives up on a connection attempt that hangs for 12 s and tries again, and stays smooth while catching up a far-behind thread.
- A malformed compressed message can no longer exhaust memory on the phone or desktop.

## Known limitations

- Reconnect on 3G is still above 1.3 s: opening the connection itself takes three round trips, which only a different relay transport could shorten.
- Copying a very long thread's full history in the background still takes about a minute on 3G.
- The timings come from PandaCode's own benchmark (`docs/connect-benchmark.md`), not from a real mobile network.
- Both computers need this version for the speed-ups; older versions keep working at the old speed.
