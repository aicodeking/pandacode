# Rank your models

## New

- **Rank models.** Settings → Agent teams → Model routing has a tier list: drag your models into S, A, B, C and D rows, or leave them Unranked. Chips show each model's harness logos; you can also move them with the keyboard (Enter to pick up, ↑/↓ or S/A/B/C/D/U to move) or a **Move to…** menu, and **Reset** clears every rank.
- **Your ranks steer Panda Router.** A ranked model's predicted chance of success is 70% its tier and 30% the router's own rating, at every difficulty. Its rank also decides the escalation order and the "never step down" floor when a worker fails. One rank covers the same model on every harness. A model the router has no rating for becomes pickable once you rank it.
- Routing results and team notes show each model's tier (`tier S`).

## Known limitations

- A rank is a strong weight, not an override. A stronger model you left unranked can still beat a weaker model you ranked S. Rank both if you want your order to decide.
- Paused models and models switched off in Models for teams don't appear in the ranker while they're unavailable. Their ranks are kept.
