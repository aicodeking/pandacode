# A full side thread in By the way

## Changed

- **The By the way panel looks like the main thread.** Side threads now show tool activity, thinking, live progress, attachment chips and message actions (copy, fork, edit, retry) exactly as the conversation does.
- **Change the side thread's model and settings.** The panel has its own model, reasoning effort, connection and permission controls, saved on the side thread only. Pick them before the first side message: they start from the main thread's choices and apply when the side thread is created.
- **Queue, steer and stop on the side.** Enter sends, or queues while the side agent works; ⌘/Ctrl Enter steers; Stop stops only the side thread. Approvals and agent questions are answered in the panel.
- **Slash commands and @ mentions in the side prompt.** `/model` and `/permissions` open the panel's own pickers, and `/rename`, `/init` and `/recap` act on the side thread. Commands that need the full thread, such as `/pwd`, say to open the side thread as a full thread.

## Known limitations

- The side prompt has no attachments, Team button or token saver.
- Steers sent to the side thread don't include the main-thread context.
- In a narrow panel the model name is shortened while the side agent works.
