# A simpler sidebar: Finished, Settled, and no project folders

## New

- **Finished.** "Needs review" is now **Finished**. A task whose agent is done stays here until you settle it, however long ago it finished and whether or not you have opened it.
- **Unseen marker.** A task that finished while you weren't looking (another thread was open, PandaCode was in the background, or you were on the board) shows a dot and a bold title until you open it.
- **Fold any section.** Active, Finished, Chats and Tasks fold from their headings, and stay folded after a restart. A thin line runs from each label to its count.

## Changed

- **No project folders with All projects.** The sidebar lists Pinned, Active, Finished, Chats and Tasks, with Settled at the bottom. Each row shows its project's icon. Pick a project at the top to see only its tasks. Remove project now lives in Settings → Projects.
- **Pinned comes first**, above Active.
- **Completed is now Settled**, in the sidebar, the board and the task menus.
- **The archive is gone.** Tasks no longer archive themselves after 7 days, and the archive button and view are removed. Tasks you archived before are moved to Settled when PandaCode starts. Reopen one to bring it back.
- The "N more in other projects" line under a filtered list is removed, and the Settled section no longer sits on a tinted block.
- The Sort and view menu drops Group and Sort projects; "Show per section" sets how many tasks Chats and Tasks show before "Show more".

## Known limitations

- With many tasks that are neither finished nor settled, the Tasks section can get long; fold it, or pick one project at the top.
