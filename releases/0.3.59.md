# Teams pick the right model for each job

## New

- **Model routing with Panda Router.** A team lead can now let PandaCode choose each worker's model. PandaCode reads the assignment, estimates how hard it is and what skills it needs, and picks the cheapest model likely to succeed. It chooses from every model of every coding tool you have installed. Leads ask for it with `profileId: "auto"`, or compare picks first with the new `route_models` tool. If you name models in your message, the lead uses those.
- **It runs on your computer.** Routing is built into the app (a small ONNX model plus a deterministic matcher, ported from [PandaOrchestrator](https://github.com/aicodeking/PandaOrchestrator)), so no Python is needed and nothing about your task leaves your machine. A route takes a few milliseconds.
- **Settings → Agents → Model routing.** Turn routing on or off and choose a mode: Cheapest that passes (default, with an adjustable minimum chance of success, 80% by default), Balanced or Best quality. The section shows the profiler's status and has a Try it box that shows which model a task would get, with its alternatives.
- **Teams see all your models.** The automatic team used to offer at most 24 models (6 per tool), so models like Muse or newer Kimi releases could be missing. The router now gets every installed model, and the list is rebuilt each time you turn a team on.

## Privacy

- The first time you turn on a team with routing on, or click Download or Route in Settings, PandaCode downloads the 35 MB profiler once from GitHub (`raw.githubusercontent.com`, a pinned commit, verified by SHA-256). It is never downloaded at launch. Until it is ready, a keyword-based fallback picks models, so teams never wait.

## Known limitations

- Model skill scores in the router's cards are estimates, and many cards for coding-tool models are new estimates by PandaCode. Models without a card are listed as unrated and never picked automatically.
- The profiler runs on Apple-silicon Macs (macOS 13.4 or later), Windows and arm64 Linux. Intel Macs and x86_64 Linux use the keyword-based fallback, because no compatible ONNX Runtime build exists for them yet, and never download the profiler.
- A team that was already on before this update sees the full model list after you turn Team off and on again.
- The download is about 18 MB larger on platforms that include ONNX Runtime.
- 0.3.58 was tagged but never published: its Intel Mac and x86_64 Linux builds failed to link ONNX Runtime. 0.3.59 is the same release with that fixed.
