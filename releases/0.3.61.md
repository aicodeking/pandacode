# A calmer, flatter PandaCode

## New

- **Frosted thread header.** The bar with the project name, Run and the other actions now floats over the conversation. Messages scroll underneath it, blurred, and its bottom edge fades out softly instead of ending in a hard line. Nothing is hidden behind it when the thread isn't scrolled, and find in thread and keyboard focus always land below it.
- **Floating Architect & workers bar.** The team bar sits at the top right as frosted glass over the conversation. It no longer draws an empty strip across the thread.
- **Redesigned model picker.** Harnesses run down the left side, with Recent and Teams at the top, and each model takes one line. Search shows which harnesses have matches, and reasoning effort is a row of buttons. Tab moves between the parts, and Esc first clears the search, then closes.
- **View menu in the sidebar.** Conversation, Board and GitHub moved from the header to one icon button next to the PandaCode name, which opens a menu. Board and Conversation are also listed in the command palette.
- **Completed stays visible.** The Completed section is docked at the bottom of the sidebar, above Bots, so many running threads never push it out of sight. Opened, it scrolls in its own panel.

## Improved

- **Flat look across the app:** no gradients or borders. Areas are set apart by background shade, spacing and rounded corners. Menus, dialogs and panels over a wallpaper keep their blur.
- **Consistent sizing:** one set of text sizes, spacing, corner radii and icon sizes throughout. Small icons are drawn with heavier lines so they stay crisp.
- **Diffs:** long lines scroll sideways with a visible scrollbar instead of breaking mid-word.
- **Clearer controls:**
  - The send button rests dimmed while the prompt is empty.
  - Secondary buttons, dropdowns and switches that are turned off stay visible in light mode.
  - Settings has distinct icons for each section and fits narrow windows.
- **Launcher:** it follows your accent colour and light or dark theme live, and shows errors in red.

## Fixed

- Loading earlier messages at the top of a thread no longer shifts what you were reading.
