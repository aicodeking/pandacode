# Subagents and teams always report back, with no capacity limits

## Fixed

- **Background subagents come back to the main agent.** A run used to end at Claude's first result, which killed subagents still working. It now stays open until their reports arrive, including several finishing together and nested subagents. If a report truly can't arrive, the thread says so.
- **Finished workers no longer stay on "Working".** A team that reached its history size could not save, so its finished workers never settled, and that error stopped scheduling for every other team. The size cap is gone, and one team's error no longer affects others.
- **The lead always hears back.** The round limit no longer keeps a lead from reviewing finished work. Every 30 seconds PandaCode also settles a worker whose run has gone without reporting and delivers any wake-up it missed.
- **A rate limit on the lead no longer stops the team.** A failed lead turn retries while workers keep running. A team waiting out a usage limit says so, with its reset time, instead of showing "Working" for hours.
- **Empty turns no longer count as finished.** On a resumed session a leftover background task could end a run before your message was answered. A worker that ends with no reply is now asked once to report, then settles as failed so the lead can act.
- **Closing your laptop** no longer fails live team work as silent.
- **Two teams in one checkout** can no longer deadlock waiting on each other's files.
- **File scopes** accept paths like `app/blog/[slug]/page.tsx` and `src/`.

## Changed

- **No more capacity limits in teams:** team history, shared notes, file scopes, rounds, assignments, batch size, sub-workers, follow-up attempts and saved setups. Messages and assignments can be up to 16 MiB, though models have their own, lower limits. The Run limits fields are gone from team setup.
- **Long results come back in pages,** so large worker reports, notes and messages are never cut off for good.
- **No more cap on subagents.** Every child is listed, and the panel loads a child's full detail when you open it.
- **"Unlimited" workers now means it on macOS:** PandaCode raises the open-file limit at startup, which used to stop at about 45 workers.
- **Codex subagents:** PandaCode lifts Codex's own subagent thread limit unless your Codex config sets one.
- **Phones and tasks on other computers:** team pauses now show their reason and a Resume button. Every worker and subagent stays listed, with a note when details were shortened to fit.

## Known limitations

- These stay on purpose: a failed assignment is replaced at most twice, sub-workers can't start their own, a silent run stops after 20 minutes, a lead with no progress after 8 wake-ups pauses once nothing is running, and named teams list up to 24 models.
- A shell left running by a subagent, such as a dev server, holds the run for at most 5 minutes. The main agent is then told, so a longer background job inside a subagent loses its report.
- A message sent with Enter waits while background subagents run. ⌘Enter steers right away.
- Codex's lifted thread limit was confirmed by reading its config back on versions from 0.141 to 0.162, not with a real spawn past the old limit. A Codex subagent still running when its main turn ends loses its report, with a note in the thread.
- General host tools such as `pandacode_cloud_cli` still time out at 60 seconds.
- A very large team costs more to save and display, since each save rewrites the whole team.
