# Desktop pandas

## Added

- PandaCode now opens with a panda animation that hands off to the sidebar wordmark. Click the wordmark to replay it; typing or clicking keeps the workspace immediately usable.
- A small panda beside Settings offers a star and opens the PandaCode repository when clicked. Its request is also available through keyboard focus.
- Both pandas follow the app theme and accessibility settings. Reduced motion keeps the branding static, and the header adapts to narrow sidebars and Mac window controls.

## Known limitations

- Desktop behavior is checked in Chrome and WebKit browser previews. Packaged native installers have not been manually tested on real hardware for this release.
