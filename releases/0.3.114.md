# Much faster long threads

## Improved

- **Long threads open, switch and scroll quickly.** A thread now draws only its newest turns and brings older ones in as you scroll up, so opening or switching to a thread with hundreds of turns no longer freezes the window. Scrolling back keeps the place you were reading, and rows far from view are trimmed once you return to the bottom.
- **Sending is fast after scrolling back.** Sending a message no longer redraws everything already on screen.
- **Switching back to a thread reuses its rows**, including the folds you had opened.
- **Find in thread covers turns that are loaded but not drawn.** It counts matches in replies and tool details before they are on screen, and draws the one you jump to.

## Known limitations

- Find searches the turns already loaded in the app. Older pages that have not been fetched yet are not searched.
- The timing improvements were measured in browser tests on a heavily loaded machine, not on each installer.
