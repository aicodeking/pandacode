# Team workers that never wait

## New

- **Team workers no longer stop to wait for approvals.** Workers dispatched by a team lead run with full access, and their approval prompts and questions are answered automatically under the lead's authority. Tools that always ask (such as deploys and scheduling) are declined with guidance instead of hanging. A request a worker can't settle ends that worker with a reason the lead can read, rather than leaving it stuck.
- **Reasoning effort per worker.** A lead can set the reasoning effort for each worker when it dispatches or follows up, fitted to what the chosen model supports.
- **A compact Workers panel.** Workers are listed by status in a short list, with a tally at the top (for example "3 working · 2 done"), an effort chip on each row, a header that stays in view while you scroll and full keyboard support. Failed workers and reviewers below the target score are marked clearly.

## Changed

- **Side threads stay out of teams.** A By the way (`/btw`) side thread starts without your saved default team and never inherits a worker's access or a lasting grant.
- **Tighter rules for remote devices, bots and schedules around teams.** Because team workers run with full access, these now need the right permission before they can drive a thread that leads a team:
  - A phone or other remote device needs **Full access for remote tasks** turned on to start a team, send to a team lead, approve its requests, answer its questions, archive or reopen it, edit its goal, queue to it or resume it.
  - A bot needs Full mode to run team turns or approve a team lead's requests from chat. Declining and cancelling still work from anywhere.
  - A schedule last saved from a remote device skips its run, with a recorded reason, when its thread now leads a team and the switch is off.

## Upgrading

- Earlier versions didn't record which device saved a schedule, so after updating, schedules you made before 0.3.118 are treated as saved remotely. They run as before unless their thread leads a team and **Full access for remote tasks** is off. In that case the schedule shows why it skipped; open it on the desktop and save it once to run it again.

## Known limitations

- The changes were checked with unit tests, the browser suites for the Workers panel and side threads, and the full Rust suite. They have not been clicked through by hand in the built app on every platform.
