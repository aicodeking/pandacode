# A cleaner top edge

## Improved

- **No top bar.** The conversation now runs right up to the top of the window. There's no blurred band, no line and no project name above it. As you scroll, text softly fades out at the top edge.
- **No scrollbar in the conversation.** The thread still scrolls with the trackpad, mouse wheel and keyboard, but no scrollbar is drawn beside it.
- Bot conversations keep their name, status, pause and settings controls at the top.
