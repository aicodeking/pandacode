# Earlier steps stay folded

## Fixed

- **Earlier tool calls no longer reopen.** When an agent replied and then went back to work, the tool calls from before the reply opened up again, cards and all. They now fold to a one-line summary once the agent writes past them and stay folded while new steps arrive. Click the summary to open them; they stay open if you do.

## Known limitations

- The Board view and the "expand activity" setting keep every step open, as before.
