# The response card shrinks back, and everyone gets to see the live scenes

## Changed

- **Wallpapers start switched off once for everyone.** The thread now shows its live scene after this update, even if you had a wallpaper on. Your scene, set, visibility and blur are kept: turn on **Wallpapers** in Settings → Appearance to bring them back, and they stay on.

## Fixed

- **The response card no longer stays long.** It now shrinks back as tool outputs fold, whether they fold by themselves or you collapse them, so a long output never leaves a blank band inside the card.
- **Earlier tool steps fold again.** Steps the agent has already talked past collapse to their summary while it works, as they did before 0.3.94.
- **The thread stays still while the card shrinks.** What sits above the card no longer slides down. The freed space sits under the card until new content fills it or you scroll up. A card taller than the window moves down only until its top comes into view, so a fold never leaves the window empty.
- **Show N more** now lines up with its section heading in the sidebar.

## Known limitations

- After a long fold there can be empty space between the card and the prompt box until the agent writes more or you scroll up.
- These changes were tested in the Chrome and WebKit previews, not yet in the installed app with a real agent running.
