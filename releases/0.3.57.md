# Bots, rebuilt: statuses, channels, backup models and a cleaner chat

## New

- **A Bots sidebar that tells you what each bot is doing.** Every bot shows one status on its avatar: needs you (with a Review or Answer chip), usage limit (with the reset time), working, unread replies, replied or failed. Bots group into Needs you, Pinned, Bots and Hidden (paused bots look asleep). Right-click to pin, edit, duplicate, hide or delete, and drag to reorder. Placeholder rows show while bots load.
- **A simpler way to make a bot.** Pick a colour and face, give it a name and say what it should help with, choose a model, and press Create bot. Suggestion cards fill the form for common jobs (research, inbox triage, code review and more).
- **Settings that save as you type.** Profile now has Brain (model, reasoning and an ordered list of backup models) and Rules (Workspace only, Ask first or Full access; notifications; whether messages sent while the bot works are queued or steer the running turn).
- **A chat view made for bots.** Your messages sit on the right and the bot's on the left. Hover a message to copy it, reply to it or react. While the bot works you see its face, a short "Working on it…" label and the elapsed time; a finished turn folds into "N steps · time". Changing the model mid-turn shows "After this turn".
- **Backup models.** When a model hits its usage limit or errors before doing anything, the bot moves to the next backup model automatically, or asks first, and the chat notes the switch ("Usage limit reached. Switched to …"). It switches back after the limit resets.
- **Shared channels.** Create a channel like #launch with several bots and a lead. Posts go to the lead or to the bots you @mention, with the channel's recent history; replies come back with each bot's name and avatar. Stop, Continue and Reassign work on running, stopped and failed posts. Deleting a channel archives it, and Restore brings it back.
- **Bots can message each other.** A bot can send a message to another bot; both chats show the exchange and its status, and the answer comes back as "Update from …". Chains stop after three hops.
- **Queue controls.** Queued messages can be steered, edited, deleted and reordered by dragging or with Alt+↑/↓.

## Fixed

- Messages with images or moodboard items sent to a bot now include its brief and memories.
- A bot's hidden brief no longer shows in the sidebar preview.
- Pausing a bot shows an error if it fails.

## Known limitations

- After a restart, channel work that was running is marked failed, and channel work that was waiting stays queued until you resume the bot.
- The per-bot Telegram, Slack, Discord, WhatsApp and web chat settings are now called Connections.
