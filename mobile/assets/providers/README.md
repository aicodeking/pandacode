# Harness logos

Byte-for-byte copies of the repository's `src/assets/providers/` files. Their sources,
retrieval dates and checksums are in [docs/brand-assets.md](../../../docs/brand-assets.md).
They identify the harness a task runs on; they are not PandaCode branding, imply no
endorsement, and remain their owners' marks. Keep them proportional and in their own colors
(monochrome marks are only tinted to the text color for contrast). Update both copies together.
