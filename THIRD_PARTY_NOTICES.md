- Used in: `src-tauri/src/voice/local/catalog.json`, generated by `scripts/speech-catalog.mjs`

The built-in local speech model catalog (model names, descriptions, repositories, revisions, file sizes and SHA-256 digests, language lists, capabilities, licence identifiers and benchmark results) is derived from this package's `catalog.json`. The catalog lists models; it does not ship their weights. Each model is downloaded from its own repository only when you choose it, and each is covered by its own licence, which PandaCode shows next to the model in Settings → Voice.

## Speech models

The models in the built-in catalog are not bundled; each is downloaded from its Hugging Face repository only when you choose it and is covered by its own licence, shown with a link on its card in Settings → Voice. `scripts/speech-catalog.mjs` leaves out models whose licences restrict commercial use or impose use restrictions unsuited to a general-purpose app: non-commercial (CC BY-NC) models, the Moonshine Community License models and Google's MedASR (Health AI Developer Foundations terms).
